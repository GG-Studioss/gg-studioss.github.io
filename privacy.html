Privacy Policy

How GG Studio handles information in our games.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the games developed and published by GG Studio ("we", "our", or "us"), including Memory Snap, handle information when you play them on iOS (Apple App Store) or Android (Google Play).

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or home address. Our games do not require an account.

Our games use third-party services (listed in section 6) that automatically collect or process certain information to provide their services. This information may include:

  • Device identifiers (for example an app instance ID, the identifier for vendors on iOS, or the advertising ID where allowed)
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it, such as your country
  • Device information such as model, operating system, language, screen size, and connection type
  • Gameplay and app interactions, such as levels played, features used, and in-game events
  • Advertising interactions, such as ads viewed or clicked
  • Performance and diagnostic information, such as loading times
  • Purchase information, such as which in-game item was bought (never your payment details)
  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications
  • Google Play Games profile and gameplay information, where Google Play Games Services is used

The exact information processed depends on the game, your device settings, your consent choices, and the features you use.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Your game progress, settings, in-game currency, and any player name you choose in the game are saved on your device. If you delete the game, this data is deleted with it.

Features such as sharing a result or saving an image use your device's own share sheet and photo library, and only when you choose to use them. Spoken text (voice assistant) is produced by your device's built-in text-to-speech.

3. Analytics and Notifications

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how our games are played (for example which levels are hard, which features are used, and where players stop playing) so we can improve them. Firebase Analytics processes an app instance ID, device information, approximate location, and in-game events.

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to send push notifications, only if you allow notifications on your device. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

4. Advertising

Our games show advertisements through Unity Ads and, in some games, other advertising and mediation providers. Optional rewarded ads are watched only when you choose to.

Advertising providers may process device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location, device information, app and advertising interactions, and purchase information to show, measure, limit, and improve ads and to prevent fraud.

On iOS, our games do not track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. We do not access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) unless you allow it through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization in your device settings.

Depending on your location, device settings, and consent choices, advertisements may be personalized, non-personalized, or limited. Purchasing an ad-removal option turns off non-rewarded ads.

5. In-App Purchases

Our games may offer optional in-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) on iOS and by Google Play Billing on Android, using Unity In-App Purchasing. We never receive or store your payment card details or other payment credentials.

Apple or Google may process purchase information as needed to complete transactions, verify purchases, restore previous purchases, manage entitlements, and prevent fraud.

6. Third-Party Services

The following services may be used by our games. They handle information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Cloud Messaging): firebase.google.com/support/privacy, policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Ads and Unity In-App Purchasing: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Apple App Store: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Play Services, Google Play Games Services, and Google Play Billing: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Other advertising and mediation providers used by a specific game

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers.

7. Google Play Games Services

Some of our Android games may use Google Play Games Services for optional features such as achievements, leaderboards, and player sign-in. When you use these features, Google may process information associated with your Google Play Games profile and your activity in the game. We use it only to provide those features.

8. Data Retention

Data saved on your device stays there until you delete the game. Third-party services keep the information they process for the periods described in their own privacy policies (for example, Firebase Analytics keeps event data for a limited time before it is deleted).

9. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Third-party services in our games may process limited device or technical information according to their own policies and applicable laws.

If a game is directed toward children or includes children in its target audience, we configure advertising and other third-party services as required by applicable laws and platform policies.

10. Data Security

We use reputable third-party services and take reasonable steps to protect the privacy and security of our players. Because information sent over the internet cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of certain processing.

You can also control data processing on your device:

  • Turn notifications on or off in your device settings
  • On iOS, manage tracking permissions in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking
  • On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization in your device settings
  • Delete the game to remove all game data stored on your device

To request access to or deletion of data processed by our analytics or advertising partners, contact us at the email below. Please mention the game's name and your device type so we can help.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our games, third-party services, legal requirements, or our privacy practices. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
