Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how All Games, developed and published by GG Studio ("we", "our", or "us"), handles information when you use our mobile game.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect, store, or request personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, home address, or other personally identifying information through the game.

However, our game uses third-party services that may automatically collect or process certain information as necessary to provide their services.

This information may include:

  • Device identifiers and advertising identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address
  • Device type and operating system information
  • App interactions and gameplay-related activity
  • Advertising interactions
  • Diagnostic, crash, and performance information
  • Purchase and transaction-related information
  • Google Play Games profile and gameplay information when Google Play Games Services is used

The exact information processed depends on the third-party services, device settings, consent choices, and features used.

2. Advertising

Our game displays advertisements through third-party advertising services.

Advertising providers may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, app interactions, advertising interactions, diagnostic information, and similar technical information to provide, measure, limit, and improve advertisements and to prevent fraud.

Depending on your location, device settings, and consent choices, advertisements may be personalized, non-personalized, or limited.

We do not directly receive or store sensitive personal information from users for advertising purposes.

3. In-App Purchases

Our game may offer optional in-app purchases.

Purchases are processed through Google Play's billing system. We do not directly collect or store your payment card details or other payment credentials.

Google Play may process purchase information as necessary to complete transactions, verify purchases, manage entitlements, prevent fraud, and provide related services.

4. Google Play Games Services

Our game may use Google Play Games Services for optional gaming functionality, including:

  • Achievements
  • Leaderboards
  • Player sign-in
  • Other supported Google Play Games functionality

When you use these features, Google may process information associated with your Google Play Games profile and your activity within the game.

We use this functionality only to provide and operate the relevant gameplay features.

5. Third-Party Services

Our game may use third-party services for advertising, purchases, achievements, leaderboards, and related functionality.

These third-party services operate according to their own privacy policies and terms. Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies.

Services that may be used by the game include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Games Services
  • Google Play Billing
  • Advertising and mediation providers used by the game

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of the relevant third-party service providers.

6. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Third-party services integrated into the game may process limited device or technical information according to their own policies and applicable laws.

If the game is directed toward children or includes children within its target audience, we configure advertising and other third-party services as required by applicable laws and platform policies.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to use reputable third-party services and protect the privacy and security of our users.

Because information transmitted over the internet or processed by third-party services cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of certain processing.

You may also have privacy controls available through your device, Google account, Google Play account, or advertising consent settings.

Where required by applicable law, users will be provided with choices regarding personalized advertising and related data processing.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the game, third-party services, legal requirements, or our privacy practices.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
